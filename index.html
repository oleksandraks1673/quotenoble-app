How Quote.Noble works

  • Saving and sync: Quotes and jobs are saved on this device and synced to your account when online. Check the save status before changing devices. Sync runs automatically. If it needs attention, open More → Autosave.
  • Backups: Optional downloads and recovery tools are under More → Autosave → Backup & recovery options. Wait for the cloud save confirmation before clearing browser data.
  • Quotes: Choose a client, add your services, then save and share. Turn an accepted quote into a job and create an invoice when the work is complete.
  • Invoices: record deposits and payments. The balance updates itself. Anything past its due date with money still owed gets flagged overdue.
  • PDF: open any quote or invoice, tap Download PDF, and send the file to your client. (Needs internet the first time so the PDF tool can load.)
  • Billing: Open More → Billing & subscription to check your plan, update payment details, or manage your subscription.
  • Tax: set your tax rate once under More → Edit business info. Ontario HST is 13%.
