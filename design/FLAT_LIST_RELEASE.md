# Flat quote and invoice lists

User-requested follow-up removes rounded document cards, their backgrounds and shadows. Quote, invoice and recent-document lists use flat rows and thin dividers. The recent-documents container is also flat. Changes are screen-only and do not modify application scripts or data.

Production deployment: dpl_4B7LbE9bZWYx7paRx8SK62gdQvw9.
Base production deployment: dpl_6xvADL2QiXY66xWRkfBkw15hPPqz.

Local browser QA confirmed 0px document row radius, transparent background and no box shadow. Sample-data screenshots were used for visual review.
